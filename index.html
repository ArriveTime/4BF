Meet Smart

Meeting someone new? Keep it public.

Whether it's a date, new friend, activity partner or business contact, basic precautions are always a good idea.

🏙️

Meet Publicly

For an initial meeting, choose a populated public location rather than a private residence or isolated area.

📱

Tell Someone

Consider telling a trusted person where you're going and when you expect to return.

🚗

Control Your Transportation

Consider arranging transportation that allows you to leave independently whenever you choose.

🔐

Protect Personal Information

Be thoughtful about sharing your home address, financial information, passwords or other sensitive information.

💳

Be Alert To Money Requests

Be cautious if a new online contact asks for money, gift cards, financial information or unusual payments.

🍹

Stay Aware

Keep control of food and drinks and stay aware of your surroundings when meeting someone new.

🚪

You Can Leave

If a situation makes you uncomfortable, you do not need to remain. Move to a safer environment or leave.

🚨

Emergency?

If you believe you are in immediate danger in the United States, contact 911 or the appropriate local emergency service.
